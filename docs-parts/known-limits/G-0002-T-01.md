section: mpesa_analyzer
title: A signed amount stops the whole statement

SPEC.md does not say whether an amount can carry a minus sign. The parser rejects a signed amount in a `Completed` row with a `StatementError` that names the line and the column. This avoids guessing what the sign means, which could give wrong totals. No real statement was checked. If a supported layout writes `Withdrawn` as a negative number, add a made-up sample to the spec and tests, then support it.
