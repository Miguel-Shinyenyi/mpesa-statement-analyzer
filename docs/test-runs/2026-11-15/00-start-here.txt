WHICH MUSE MODEL
Muse Video, from Meta Superintelligence Labs. Muse Image makes stills and Muse Spark is the text model, so Muse Video is the one for this job.

WHAT I COULD CONFIRM AND WHAT I COULD NOT
Confirmed by Meta's own post of July 7, 2026: Muse Video is an early preview, "coming soon to creators and Meta AI". It makes sound in the same pass as the picture. Meta names two weak spots: audio and picture sync, and fast physical motion.
Reported by third parties, not by Meta: clips of about 10 seconds, text to video and image to video, a choice of 9:16 or 16:9 before you generate, and characters that drift between clips.
Not published anywhere I found: resolution, price, whether you can give it your own audio or a voice sample, and whether it takes a reference image.
Not known to me: whether you have access today. A third party said on September 22 that there was no public access and no API. I could not find a later confirmation. Check your Meta AI app or meta.ai before you plan around it.

WHAT THAT MEANS FOR YOUR VOICE
I do not expect Muse Video to use a voice sample. So the plan is: Muse makes ambience only, you record the lines, and an editor joins them. File 3 has the lines and also a voice sample to record, in case you want a separate voice tool.

IF MUSE VIDEO IS NOT AVAILABLE TO YOU
The prompts are written as plain scene descriptions with a fixed style, so they also work in other text to video tools. The stills in the stills folder can be the first frames if the tool takes an image. The SOUND tag can be dropped if the tool makes no sound. I have not tested the prompts in any tool, so expect to adjust them.

Every shot marked REAL is a screenshot or a screen recording, because Muse draws text badly. File 1 lists the recordings and the exact commands. Retake the progress page on filming day, since its numbers change with every merge.
